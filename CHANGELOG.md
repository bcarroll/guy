# Changelog

The big changes to GUY's room, newest first. The game shows the same list under Help > Change log (src/changelog.js); keep the two in step.

## 2026-11-23

### Changed

- The Learning Workstation and the Build a Bot Machine are kept after a reload, in whatever room they were moved to, and GUY uses the Workstation there.

## 2026-11-22

### Added

- Catalog search: one box looks through objects, books, records and rooms; a filter for what is in the room or can be added; C opens it.
- The Build a Bot machine is a Workshop object (Catalog > Workshop > Machines) and cannot leave that room; it stays after GAL is printed and prints new things from rubber, glass, plastic and metal.
- Help: the ❓ button (or H) explains how the game works, what you can do, what is new and this change log.
- Drumming for Robots has a Chapter 4, Slow & Latin: a half-time ballad groove and a bossa nova, learned by practising at the kit.

### Changed

- The Catalog looks and works like Help and the Skills menu: a grid of cards in sections that fold away, a Built by GUY category, and a bottom sheet on a phone.
- Skills menu: GAL really brings what GUY asked for, and he plays it when it comes.
- Every room is the same 10 m square, so the house lines up with no overlaps or gaps; a corner room has a door into each room beside it.
- New bowling pins are carried to a lane and set up there by hand.
- GAL dances in the room the stereo is in, not always in GUY's room.
- GUY and GAL wear the bass with its strap over the shoulder, and their hands close round the neck of a guitar or bass.
- GUY and GAL keep the same voice through a conversation: no more robot voice from one and natural voice from the other; once the natural voices have downloaded they wait for them instead of speaking robot.
- Locked in the Workshop, GUY and GAL go on using the rooms beside it whose doors are open, and furnish them; the message says who is shut in.
